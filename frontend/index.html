Quick Safety Tips

The Golden Rules of Cyber Safety

🚫

Never Share OTPs

No bank, government agency, or company will ever ask for your OTP, PIN, or password. Never share them.

🔗

Verify Links Before Clicking

Hover over links to check the URL. Look for misspellings like "sbi-bank.co.in" or "amaz0n.com".

📞

Verify Caller Identity

If someone claims to be from CBI/police, hang up and call the official number. Real officers don't video-call for arrests.

⏱️

Act in the Golden Hour

If scammed, call 1930 immediately. Transactions reported within the first hour have the highest chance of recovery.

📲

Only Install Official Apps

Never install APK files from WhatsApp or email. Only download apps from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

🔐

Enable Two-Factor Auth

Enable 2FA on all banking, email, and social media accounts. Use an authenticator app rather than SMS when possible.
